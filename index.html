Privacy Policy

Business: YounkerIT
Facebook Page: facebook.com/YounkerIT
Effective date: 23 October 2026

YounkerIT is a digital marketing agency offering social media branding, content creation, and hashtag research services through its website and Facebook Page. This policy explains what information we collect when you message our Page or share your details with us, and how we use it. Our Page uses an automated assistant ("YounkerIT Assistant") to answer common questions and to collect inquiry details on our behalf.

Information we collect

  • Your Facebook profile name and the messages you send to our Page.
  • Inquiry (lead) information you provide: your name, your business name, your phone or WhatsApp number, and the service you are interested in.
  • Basic conversation history, so we can follow up on your inquiry or questions.

How we use this information

  • To answer your questions about our services and how we work.
  • To contact you about your inquiry and discuss your project, including by phone or WhatsApp if you shared a number with us.
  • To follow up about an inquiry or an earlier conversation with us.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use your information for any purpose other than serving your inquiry and conversation with YounkerIT.
  • We do not collect payment card or banking credentials through this assistant. Any payment is handled directly between you and us outside this assistant.

Sharing

Your details are used only by the YounkerIT team to respond to you. Messages are processed through Meta platforms (Facebook Messenger) under Meta's own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Inquiry records are kept as long as needed to follow up with you and for business records. You can ask us at any time, through our Facebook Page inbox or by email at contact@younkerit.com, to delete your conversation data and personal information, and we will delete it unless we must keep a record for legal or accounting reasons.

Contact

For any privacy question or deletion request, message us at our Facebook Page: facebook.com/YounkerIT, or email us at contact@younkerit.com

This policy may be updated. The current version will always be available at this page.
