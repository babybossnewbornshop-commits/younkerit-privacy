Privacy Policy

Business: YounkerIT
Facebook Pages served by the assistant: facebook.com/YounkerIT and facebook.com/BabyBossNewbornShop
Effective date: 11 November 2026

YounkerIT is a digital marketing agency offering social media branding, content creation, and hashtag research services through its website and Facebook Page. YounkerIT also operates an automated assistant ("YounkerIT Assistant") that answers customers on Facebook Messenger. The assistant serves YounkerIT and other businesses that connect their Facebook Page to it. The businesses served under the assistant now are YounkerIT and Baby Boss. Additional businesses can join later by granting the app access to their Page. This policy explains what information we collect when you message one of these Pages or share your details with us, and how we use it.

Businesses served

  • YounkerIT (facebook.com/YounkerIT): the assistant answers in English and collects inquiry (lead) details.
  • Baby Boss (facebook.com/BabyBossNewbornShop): the assistant answers in Bengali as "Maya" and collects product orders.

Each business is responsible for its own customers' data. YounkerIT operates the assistant on each business's behalf and acts as a data processor for that business: it processes customer messages only as instructed by that business and only for that business's purposes. A new business joins one at a time, by granting the app access to its Page.

Information we collect

For each business connected to the assistant, we collect:

  • Your Facebook profile name and the messages you send to that business's Page.
  • The Page ID of the business's Page and your Page-scoped ID (PSID), which identifies you to that Page only.
  • Inquiry or order details you provide: your name, your business name, your phone or WhatsApp number, your email, the service or product you are interested in, your goal, and, where the business takes orders, the product, quantity, and delivery address.
  • Basic conversation history, so we can follow up on your inquiry or order.

AI processing

Messages are sent to Google Gemini, which classifies each message only, to find the matching approved answer. Gemini does not draft replies and does not generate facts. Gemini processes message content only to answer that business's customers.

How we use this information

  • To answer your questions about that business's services or products.
  • To contact you about your inquiry or order and discuss it, including by phone or WhatsApp if you shared a number with us.
  • To follow up about an inquiry, an order, or an earlier conversation with that business.
  • Only for that business. Your details are never used for another business.

Separation between businesses

Each business keeps its own Page, Page token, configuration, knowledge, records, mail identity, and log. The server routes every message by Page ID, so one business's data is never shown to another business or its customers. YounkerIT also keeps an up-to-date list of the businesses it serves, and a business's data is shared only for that business's purpose.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use your information for any purpose other than serving your inquiry or order with that business.
  • We do not share your information with another business on the platform.
  • We do not collect payment card or banking credentials through this assistant. Any payment is handled directly between you and the business outside this assistant.

Sharing

Your details are used only by that business's team and by the YounkerIT team operating the assistant for them. Messages are processed through Meta platforms (Facebook Messenger) under Meta's own privacy terms, and by Google Gemini as the AI provider named above. No other sharing.

Retention and deletion

Each business's records are kept only as long as needed to follow up with you and for business records. You can ask us at any time to delete your conversation data and personal information for that business, in any of these ways:

  • Through that business's own Facebook Page inbox.
  • By email to YounkerIT at info@younkerit.com, naming the business and the details to delete.
  • Through the business directly, which can ask YounkerIT to delete your records.

We will delete that business's records for you, including its records of your inquiry or order and its conversation logs for you, unless we must keep a record for legal or accounting reasons.

Contact

For any privacy question or deletion request, message the business's Facebook Page, or email us at info@younkerit.com
